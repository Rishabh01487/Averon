Built for the asset economy.

Three integrated layers — tokenization, financing, and capital mobility — in one protocol. Each capability is purpose-built, not bolted on.

i

Asset tokenization

Transform any verified real-world asset into programmable fractional tokens on a custom PoW blockchain with SHA-256 mining and ECDSA secp256k1 wallets.

SHA-256 · secp256k1
ii

AI verification

A five-stage AI pipeline using Gemini 2.0 Flash analyzes your documents for legitimacy, valuation, and risk — onboarding in minutes, not days.

iii

Asset-backed financing

Unlock liquidity without selling your assets. Borrowers receive fiat INR; investors participate through Averon Coin. Event-driven repayment, no lock-in.

Razorpay · UPI · Wire

02 — Process

From asset submission to capital receipt.

A five-step journey that transforms ownership documents into liquid capital. Each step is verifiable, signed, and recorded on-chain.

01

Submit your asset

Upload asset details — title, category, raise amount, listing duration — along with ownership documents (PDFs, images). SHA-256 hashes are computed for duplicate detection across the entire document corpus.

02

AI verification & valuation

Gemini 2.0 Flash analyzes your documents across five stages — ingestion, duplicate check, multimodal analysis, fraud detection, and tokenization recommendation. Confidence-scored, with a fallback engine if Gemini is unavailable.

03

Tokenize & list for funding

Verified asset is split into fractional tokens on the blockchain. Escrow account created, compliance checks run, vesting schedule applied. Asset goes live for funding.

04

Receive capital

Once fully funded, escrow releases 99% to your wallet and 1% platform fee. AC price boosts 2–5%. Payout recorded immutably on-chain. Repay on your schedule.

05

Capital recycles

Repaid capital flows back into the network, automatically redeployed to new verified opportunities through risk-tiered liquidity pools — a continuously compounding economy.
