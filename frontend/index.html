End-to-end flow

From asset submission to capital receipt.

A five-step journey that transforms ownership documents into liquid capital.

01

Submit your asset

Upload asset details — title, category, raise amount, listing duration — along with ownership documents (PDFs, images). SHA-256 hashes computed for duplicate detection.

02

AI verification & valuation

Gemini 2.0 Flash analyzes your documents across five stages — ingestion, duplicate check, multimodal analysis, fraud detection, and tokenization recommendation. Confidence-scored, with fallback engine.

03

Tokenize & list for funding

Verified asset is split into fractional tokens on the blockchain. Escrow account created, compliance checks run, vesting schedule applied. Asset goes live for funding.

04

Receive capital

Once fully funded, escrow releases 99% to your wallet and 1% platform fee. AC price boosts 2–5%. Payout recorded immutably on-chain. Repay on your schedule.

05

Capital recycles

Repaid capital flows back into the network, automatically redeployed to new verified opportunities through risk-tiered liquidity pools — a continuously compounding economy.
